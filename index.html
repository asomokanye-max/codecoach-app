Welcome to CodeCoach

ICD-10 coding practice with instant feedback. Pilot version for the School of Health Information Management, OAUTHC.

Information and consent

Study: Development and evaluation of CodeCoach, an AI-assisted ICD-10 coding practice tutor for HIM students.

Principal investigator: Dr Akingbola Sunday Omokanye, Institute of Public Health, Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife (asomokanye@oauife.edu.ng, +234 806 244 8712).

What you will do: practise coding cases in CodeCoach and take two coding tests. Your answers, time per case and hint use are recorded for the study.

Your data: your name is stored separately from your answers, which are linked only to a study number. Data are protected in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Voluntary: taking part will not affect your course grades. You may stop at any time by contacting the investigator.

Ethics: approved by the OAUTHC Health Research Ethics Committee [approval number].
